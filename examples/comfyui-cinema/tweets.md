## Technical reply

ComfyUI runs in CPU mode and talks to Rapid-MLX over localhost.

The video node uploads a keyframe, saves the job ID, then polls and downloads the MP4—with its generated audio.

Two passes. Fixed seeds. Resumable jobs. Workflow + code in the demo.
